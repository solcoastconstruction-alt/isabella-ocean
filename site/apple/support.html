Isabella Ocean for iPhone and iPad

Support

Email support@isabellaocean.app and a person will answer.

There is no sound

The games follow the Ring/Silent switch. Turn Silent mode off, turn the volume up, and check the speaker button on the game's title screen.

Where is the grown-ups' screen?

On the title screen, tap the round button with the grown-up and child, bottom left. Press and hold the button for 3 seconds, then answer the multiplication.

How do I unlock World 2?

Open the grown-ups' screen and tap "Unlock World 2". It is one payment through the App Store, not a subscription. Everything else in the game is free.

I bought World 2 and it is locked

Open the grown-ups' screen and tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account that bought it. Restoring is free.

I have a code

Open the grown-ups' screen and tap "Redeem a code", then type the code. If the code came as a link, opening the link on the iPhone or iPad works too. A code is free to redeem, and each person can use one.

I would like a refund

Apple handles refunds: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Tilt steering does not work in Splash Dash

Hold the device level and lean it left and right like a steering wheel. You can also steer with a finger.
