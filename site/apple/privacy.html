Isabella Ocean for iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

This page is about the Isabella Ocean app for iPhone and iPad, from the App Store. It is a set of ocean games for young children. We built it to collect nothing about your child or your family. This page explains exactly what happens.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no code from advertising or analytics companies. We never ask for a name, email, photo, location or any other personal information, and we never receive any.

The app does not connect to the internet by itself. The games play without a connection.

What stays on the device

Game progress (levels, stars and coins) and whether World 2 is unlocked are saved only on the device, inside the app. They are deleted when you delete the app. If you back up the device with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include them; we cannot see those backups.

Buying World 2

Unlocking World 2 is optional and happens only behind a parent gate. The purchase is made with Apple's in-app purchase, through your Apple Account.

  • Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, your Apple Account, your card or your address.
  • The app asks Apple, on the device, whether World 2 has been bought, so it can open or restore it. That answer stays on the device.
  • Apple tells us how many purchases were made and in which countries. It does not tell us who made them.
  • What Apple does with your purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Tilt steering

One game, Splash Dash, reads the device's motion sensor so your child can steer by tilting. The reading is used on the spot to move Isabella. It is not saved and it never leaves the device.

Children

The games are designed for children and collect no personal information from them. Children never see a purchase screen or a link out of the app; those sit behind a parent gate meant for adults (press and hold, then a multiplication).

Sharing

We have no personal information to share, sell or transfer, and we never will.

This website and email

These pages use no cookies and no analytics. Like every website, the company that hosts them (Cloudflare) sees the internet address of a device that asks for a page, in order to send it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use them only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Contact

Isabella Ocean for iPhone and iPad is published by the developer named as the seller on its App Store page; "we" on this page means that developer. Questions about privacy: email support@isabellaocean.app.
