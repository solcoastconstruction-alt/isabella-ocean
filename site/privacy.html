Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Isabella Ocean ("we") makes games for children aged 3 to 8 on the Solana Seeker phone. We built them to collect nothing about your child or your family. This page explains exactly what happens.

What we collect

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never ask for a name, email, photo, location or any other personal information, and we never receive any.

What stays on the phone

Game progress (levels, stars and coins) and your unlock status are saved only on the phone, inside the app. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

When a grown-up connects a wallet

Unlocking the extra levels is optional and happens only behind a parent gate. If a grown-up connects a Solana wallet:

  • The app learns that wallet's public address, which is already public on the Solana blockchain. It keeps it on the phone only.
  • To check whether the extra levels are unlocked, the app asks a Solana network server (an "RPC" server) about that address's public balances and past payments to us. Like any website, that server sees the request and the phone's internet address. We do not run that server and do not receive this information.
  • Staking and payments are blockchain transactions you approve in your own wallet. They are public on the Solana blockchain by design.

Children

The games are designed for children and collect no personal information from them. Children never see wallet or payment screens; those sit behind a parent gate meant for adults.

Sharing

We have no personal information to share, sell or transfer, and we never will.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us through our listing on the Solana dApp Store.
