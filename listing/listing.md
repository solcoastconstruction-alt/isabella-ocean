---

Isabella Ocean is nine calm, colourful ocean games for children aged about 3 to 8, made for the Solana Seeker. Your child never needs to read: every game is played with taps, drags, swipes or a tilt of the phone.

THE NINE GAMES
• Isabella the Mermaid (5–8): steer Isabella through the sea, find the key and open the treasure chest. 20 levels in two worlds.
• Bubble Party (3–5): drag each sea creature's bubble home to its picture on the sand.
• Shell Match (5–8): a memory game with clam shells.
• Coral Maze (5–8+): swipe Isabella out of coral mazes with keys, gates and currents. 20 Easy and 20 Hard levels.
• Sea Words (5–8): a word search where every word comes with its picture.
• Treasure Blocks (5–8+): slide and turn the sinking sea blocks to fill a row.
• Sea Jigsaw (3–8): 30 sea pictures cut into real jigsaw pieces, from 4 pieces up to 40.
• Splash Dash (5–8): tilt the phone to steer Isabella along the surface. 20 courses.
• Sea Catch (3–8): slide Isabella left and right to catch the falling sea friends.

WHAT IS FREE
Eight of the nine games are free from start to finish. So is World 1 of Isabella the Mermaid, levels 1 to 10. The free games play offline.

THE ONE THING TO UNLOCK
World 2 of Isabella the Mermaid, levels 11 to 20, is the only paid part. A grown-up unlocks it in one of two ways:

1. Stake 1 SOL, refundable any time. You deposit about 1.01 SOL into the Isabella Ocean stake pool and hold the pool's OCEAN tokens in your own wallet. Take your SOL back whenever you like: instantly for a 0.3% fee, or free in about 2 days. World 2 locks again when you do. You do not earn staking rewards on this SOL; the pool keeps them, and that is how the games are paid for. Your SOL's value still moves with SOL's price.

2. Pay US$4.99 once, with no subscription. Pay in any Jupiter-verified token your wallet holds, SKR included, and see the exact amount in your wallet before you approve. A paid unlock never expires, and Restore purchase brings it back after a reinstall with the same wallet.

KIDS CAN'T PAY
Every wallet action sits behind a parent gate: press and hold, then answer a multiplication. After that, your wallet still asks you to approve.

PRIVACY AND ADS
No ads. No accounts or sign-in. No analytics or tracking. Nothing about your child is collected, and game progress stays on the phone. The app goes online only to reach the Solana network and Jupiter, to make the unlock and to check it.

Runs in landscape, full screen.

Support: support@isabellaocean.app

## Notes for the reviewer

---

Eight of the nine games are free and need no wallet. The only paid part is World 2 of Isabella the Mermaid (levels 11 to 20). There are no accounts and no sign-in.

To reach the unlock: on the title screen, tap the small round grown-ups button in the bottom-left corner. Hold the button for 3 seconds, then answer the multiplication (this is the parent gate). Tap "Unlock World 2".

Way 1, "Stake 1 SOL": the wallet deposits 1.01 SOL into the app's own stake pool (the standard SPL stake-pool program; pool HoG8qP3nFU5erDhwPkW7MYW2ZDK9yj69mifBUmQd7Pnk) and receives 1.01 OCEAN pool tokens. World 2 opens. To take the SOL back: the grown-ups button, the parent gate, "Get my SOL back now". The wallet receives the SOL less a 0.3% fee and World 2 locks again. A full stake-and-exit round trip costs about 0.003 SOL.

Way 2, "Pay US$4.99 once": choose any token the wallet holds; the app shows the exact amount and the wallet asks for approval. World 2 stays open. After a reinstall, the grown-ups button, the parent gate and "Restore purchase" bring it back with the same wallet at no cost.

All three were run on mainnet on a Seeker with the Seed Vault Wallet on 8 October 2026.

Support: support@isabellaocean.app
